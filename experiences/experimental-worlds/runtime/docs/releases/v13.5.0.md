# Horde Studio 13.5.0

Horde Studio 13.5 gives Virtual Humans a public life beyond private messages. Social profiles now carry authored history, autonomous activity, media, audience relationships and persistent player interactions without turning every timer tick into a paid model call.

## Social lives for Virtual Humans

- Added a prominent Social Media surface directly inside Virtual Human chat, with dedicated Feed and Gallery views.
- Social feeds can be paused independently while their existing profile and gallery remain accessible.
- Creators can author a starting profile manually or ask the active text model to draft editable status and photo posts.
- Added Instagram-style, microblog, Facebook-style, subscriber and custom profile formats.
- Added configurable posting frequency, photo ratio, writing voice, audience, monetization, access rules and 21 selectable content categories.
- Optional autonomous posting is grounded in the human's current life, schedule, location, outfit, social circle and recent events.
- Tiny Brain can act as a low-cost private posting gate; it decides whether a grounded moment is worth sharing but never writes canonical state itself.

## Relationship-aware social interaction

- Players can follow, subscribe, like, comment, tip with fictional roleplay currency and unlock paid posts when creators enable those mechanics.
- Social roles distinguish strangers, followers, mutuals, friends, subscribers and VIP subscribers.
- Likes, comments, subscriptions, tips and unlocks persist in the active timeline and become concise context for future conversations.
- Virtual Humans may notice an interaction, ignore it, mention it later or allow it to affect the relationship naturally.

## Media and gallery experience

- Added responsive full-size photo modals with a blurred backdrop, preserved aspect ratio, captions and inline comments.
- Gallery access remains available when autonomous posting is disabled.
- Added upload and generation support for authored starter posts.
- Bundled Ashlyn now includes 21 starter posts: 15 photos, ordinary status updates and meme-style posts.
- Portable builds now include relative bundled-human media without including heavyweight marketing assets.

## Virtual Human multimodal improvements

- Players can send supported images and voice notes directly to a Virtual Human.
- Persona avatars can be shown to the human once and retained as visual memory instead of being resent on every turn.
- Text, image and speech providers remain independently selectable; unsupported input controls are disabled clearly.
- Local Tiny Brain support now contributes optional social gating and life-beat assistance without replacing the primary conversation model.

## Reliability fixes

- Repaired duplicate social-post identities that could make every gallery thumbnail open the same photo.
- Existing saved timelines are repaired without detaching healthy likes, comments, tips or unlocks.
- New manual, AI-generated and bundled starter posts now place their unique identity before length-capped metadata.
- Comments are attached to the selected post and are available to the next relevant model call.
- Added cache-version updates so browsers load the repaired social implementation immediately.

## Validation

- All 15 bundled Ashlyn feed images opened as 15 distinct assets.
- All 15 gallery thumbnails opened the matching image, including after a full application reload.
- Comment targeting was verified against two different posts.
- All bundled social images were decoded successfully.
- Browser console, JavaScript syntax and Git whitespace checks passed.
