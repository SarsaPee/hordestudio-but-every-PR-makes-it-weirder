🖥️ Inject System Instruction

What is this for?
System injections allow you to steer the AI quietly without your character having to speak or act out of place. It functions as a direct command to the Dungeon Master or the underlying engine.

Examples:
• "The weather suddenly turns violent and a storm begins."
• "Make the NPC act highly suspicious and avoid eye contact."
• "A distant explosion rocks the building."
